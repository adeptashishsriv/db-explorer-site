Data Deletion Policy for DB Explorer

Last updated: May 20, 2024

This page outlines the process for requesting the deletion of your personal data from DB Explorer services.

Your Right to Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data that we have collected, subject to certain exceptions required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

Types of Data We Hold

As described in our Privacy Policy, we may collect:

  • Email address (if you sign in)
  • First name and last name (if you sign in)
  • Usage Data (e.g., IP address, browser type, pages visited)
  • Information from Third-Party Social Media Services (e.g., Google, Facebook, GitHub) if you use them to sign in.

For blog authors, this also includes any content you have published (posts, comments) associated with your account.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your personal data, please send an email to ashish@astroadept.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

In your email, please include the following information:

  • Your full name.
  • The email address associated with your DB Explorer account.
  • A clear statement requesting the deletion of your personal data.
  • (Optional) Any specific data you wish to have deleted, or if you request full account deletion.

Verification Process

To protect your privacy and security, we will need to verify your identity before processing your deletion request. We may contact you at the email address provided to confirm your request or ask for additional information to verify your identity.

What Happens After Your Request

Once your identity is verified and your request is confirmed:

  • We will delete your personal data from our active databases within 30 days.
  • Any content you have published (e.g., blog posts) may be anonymized or removed, depending on the nature of the content and our legal obligations.
  • Residual copies of your data may remain in our backup systems for a limited period, but they will be isolated from active processing and eventually purged in accordance with our data retention policies.
  • We will notify you once your data deletion request has been completed.

Exceptions

Please note that we may be unable to delete certain data if it is necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation.
  • Exercise the right of freedom of expression and information.
  • For reasons of public interest in the area of public health.
  • For archiving purposes in the public interest, scientific or historical research purposes or statistical purposes.
  • For the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Data Deletion Policy or your data deletion request, you can contact us:

  • By email: ashish@astroadept.com
